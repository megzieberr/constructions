How the tools work

Compass

  • Drag the top handle to swing it and draw an arc. Swing all the way round for a circle.
  • Drag the needle arm to move the whole compass. The needle clicks onto points and crossings.
  • Drag the pencil arm to open or close it. It clicks onto points too, so you can copy a length.
  • Tap the lock to keep the width. Then the pencil arm only turns it.

Ruler

  • Drag the middle to move it. Its edge clicks onto points; an orange ring shows where it is stuck.
  • Turn it with a round knob. It turns around the point it is stuck on and clicks onto the next point.
  • Start your pen on an edge to draw along it. The label shows the length.

Pen, line and eraser

  • Line draws a straight line without the ruler. Its ends click onto points.
  • Eraser: touch or rub across a line and the whole line goes. Holding the S Pen button may erase too.
  • Undo and redo go one step at a time. Clear can be undone as well.
